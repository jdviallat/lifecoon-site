User Guide

Everything you can do

Lifecoon brings all your health together in one place. This guide shows you, feature by feature, what the app can do and how to do it, step by step.

Tip: Lifecoon is organized into 5 spaces at the bottom of the screen — Home, Tracking, Treatments, Medical and Records — plus the ⚙️ Settings wheel at the top. Everything starts there.


🚀 1 · Getting started

The first time you open it, Lifecoon guides you through a few screens: introduction, consent, then app protection. No account, no sign-in, no email address.

Protect the app

You can lock Lifecoon with a PIN code and/or Face ID / fingerprint. The app re-locks automatically when it goes to the background.

How to do it
  1. On first launch, choose a 4–8 digit code, then enable biometrics if you wish.
  2. To change it later: ⚙️ Settings › Security — set/remove the PIN, enable Face ID / fingerprint.

Fill in your profile

How to do it
  1. Open ⚙️ Settings › Profile.
  2. Enter your name, height, date of birth and a photo if you like (it appears on the home screen).
  3. Height is used to automatically calculate your BMI when you weigh yourself.
Family: you can track several people using profiles (⚙️ Settings › Profiles). Each profile has its own measurements, treatments and appointments.

🏠 2 · The dashboard

The Home screen is your daily summary: latest values, next appointment, medications to take, trends and vigilance alerts.

  • Cards of your latest measurements with color coding (based on your thresholds)
  • Vigilance banner if a trend deserves attention
  • Next appointment and today's doses
  • Tip of the day and a "Read my day" button (spoken summary)
How to do it
  1. Tap a card (Blood pressure, Blood sugar, Weight…) to open its detailed tracking directly.
  2. Tap the "+ Add a measurement" button to enter a value without leaving the screen.

❤️ 3 · Measurements & tracking

Track blood pressure (with pulse), blood sugar, weight/BMI, SpO₂, temperature, heart rate — and create your own measurements. Each tracker has its own curve and history.

Record a measurement

How to do it
  1. From Home ("+ Add a measurement") or the Tracking tab.
  2. Choose the type (blood pressure / blood sugar / weight…), enter the value — or use voice input.
  3. For blood sugar, specify the moment (fasting, before/after a meal, bedtime); for blood pressure, the app also records the pulse.
  4. Save: the value appears in the curve right away.

Read and correct your history

How to do it
  1. Tracking tab › choose the measurement.
  2. Slide across the chart to see each point; change the period to 7 / 30 / 90 days.
  3. In the list below, tap a row to edit or delete it.

Customize alert thresholds

The colors (normal / to watch / high) rely on your thresholds, not on a diagnosis.

How to do it
  1. ⚙️ Settings › Thresholds.
  2. Adjust the upper values for blood pressure, fasting and post-meal blood sugar, and the blood sugar unit (g/L, mmol/L or mg/dL).

💊 4 · Treatments & reminders

Manage your medications, their schedules and their intake conditions, get reminders and track your adherence.

Add a medication

How to do it
  1. Treatments tab › "+".
  2. Enter the name, the dosage, the intake times, and a photo of the box if you like.
  3. Open "Intake conditions" to specify: length of the course, "fasting" before/after, spacing from other medications, instructions.
  4. Add the active substance (INN) so the app can spot duplicates and interactions.
Reminders: allow notifications when first prompted. The app reminds you at the chosen time, even offline, and shows the "fasting" instruction where relevant.

Mark a dose taken & track adherence

How to do it
  1. On Home or in Treatments, check off each dose Taken ✓ / Missed.
  2. The "30-day adherence" card shows your percentage and any missed doses.

Side effects & interactions

  • Note a side effect per medication (mild / moderate / strong + note) — included in the report.
  • The app flags duplicate substances and schedule conflicts (non-blocking warnings; to discuss with your doctor).

📅 5 · Medical tab

Since version 2.4.0, the Medical tab brings together everything about your caregivers and deadlines: appointments, doctors, pharmacies and exams.

Appointments

How to do it
  1. Medical › Appointments tab › "+".
  2. Enter the reason, date, place, practitioner and a reminder.
  3. Option: sync to your calendar (phone calendar or a CalDAV account configured in Settings).

My doctors & pharmacies

  • Doctor directory: clickable phone and address, linked to your appointments.
  • Pharmacy directory + a pharmacy shopping list (medications to pick up, supplies to restock), shareable.

Prescriptions & exams

  • Renewable prescriptions: validity, photos, linked medications, expiry alert.
  • Reminders for exams you shouldn't forget.

📥 6 · Automatic imports

Lifecoon can fill in your data for you from a prescription, a report or a connected device. Everything happens on the device, offline.

Import a prescription (PDF or photo)

The app reads the prescription and offers to create the medications and their dosage automatically.

How to do it
  1. Treatments tab or the "Scan" button › choose "Prescription".
  2. Take a photo or select a PDF.
  3. Check the proposed medications and schedules, correct if needed, then confirm.
Always check what is recognized before confirming: automatic reading can make mistakes. It does not replace your doctor's prescription.

Import an exam or a lab test

How to do it
  1. Tracking › Import tab (or from "Custom measurements").
  2. Choose a PDF report or a photo; the app extracts the values.
  3. Assign each value to a measurement (blood sugar, weight, or a custom measurement), choose the date, confirm.

Bluetooth connected devices

Import automatically from a compatible blood pressure monitor, glucose meter or scale (standard Bluetooth profiles).

How to do it
  1. Tracking › Connected devices tab.
  2. Allow Bluetooth, start the scan, select your device and follow the measurement.

Apple Health / Health Connect

How to do it
  1. ⚙️ Settings › Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS).
  2. Allow reading; the import (weight) is read-only and done locally.

📄 7 · Records tab & doctor report

The Records tab gathers everything you share or keep safe: the doctor report, the emergency card, vaccinations, documents and the monthly review.

Generate the report for your doctor

This is the flagship feature: a clear PDF for the period of your choice, with summaries, charts and readings, out-of-threshold values highlighted.

How to do it
  1. Records › Report tab.
  2. Choose the period (7 days, 30 days, 3 months, 12 months, all).
  3. Generate, then share the PDF (email, printing, messaging…). You can also export to Excel or as grid tables (logbook style).

Emergency card

Blood type, allergies, emergency contact and current treatments — useful for first responders.

How to do it
  1. Records › Emergency card tab; fill in your information.
  2. Enable "Show without unlocking" so a button appears on the lock screen (read-only).

Vaccinations, documents & monthly review

  • Vaccination record with reminders, included in the emergency card and the report.
  • Documents (photo or PDF) with offline OCR to find the text again.
  • Monthly review "Your health month": averages, trends, adherence, with PDF export and a reminder on the 1st of the month.
  • Hospital handover sheet as a PDF (identity, allergies, treatments, vaccines, values, doctors).

📈 8 · Advanced analysis

Custom measurements

Create your own trackers (peak flow, a lab value…) with unit, decimals and thresholds.

How to do it
  1. ⚙️ Settings › Custom measurements › create a type (name, unit, thresholds).
  2. It then appears in the Tracking tab with its curve and history.

Pain tracking

How to do it
  1. Tracking › Pain tab.
  2. Tap the affected body area, set the intensity (0–10), add a note. The trend is included in the report.

Correlations & printable pill organizer

  • Correlations: overlay two measurements and spot the links (with treatment start/end markers).
  • Weekly pill organizer: a grid of moments × 7 days, exportable as a landscape PDF to print.
  • Consultation preparation: write down your questions and a summary to take with you (PDF).

🔔 9 · Alerts & accessibility

Smart alerts

The app watches your trends and notifies you for guidance only: rising morning blood pressure, blood sugar often above target, weight variation, drop in adherence.

How to do it
  1. ⚙️ Settings › Smart alerts to turn them on/off.

Reading comfort (seniors)

How to do it
  1. ⚙️ Settings › Display: enable "Large display" (bigger text and buttons) and "High contrast".
  2. Change the language among the 9 available in ⚙️ Settings › Language.

💾 10 · Backup & never losing your data

Your data never leaves the device: so keeping a copy is up to you. Lifecoon makes it simple and automatic.

How to do it
  1. Manual backup: ⚙️ Settings › Backup › Export creates an encrypted file (.lifecoon) that you keep wherever you want.
  2. Automatic backup: choose a folder (e.g. iCloud Drive / Files); an encrypted copy is written there regularly — it survives an uninstall.
  3. Restore: ⚙️ Settings › Backup › Import and select your .lifecoon file.
Changing phones: export a backup on the old one, install Lifecoon on the new one, then import the file. App updates keep your data.

🔒 11 · Privacy & security

  • No data sent over the Internet, no account, no ads, no trackers.
  • Encrypted database on the device, locking by PIN and biometrics.
  • You can erase everything at any time (⚙️ Settings › Data › Delete).
  • Checking for updates transmits no personal data.

Full details in the privacy policy.

❓ 12 · Frequently asked questions

Does the app replace a doctor?

No. Lifecoon is a tracking logbook. It does not make any diagnosis and does not replace medical advice. Alerts rely solely on the thresholds you define.

Is it really free?

Yes, entirely: no paid features, no subscription, no in-app purchases.

Is my data in the cloud?

No. Everything stays on your device, encrypted. The backups you create go wherever you choose.

How do I get updates?

On iPhone via the App Store. On Android via Google Play or the direct APK: the app notifies you automatically when a new version is available.
